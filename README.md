# Rootfold

**Open your RootsMagic family tree on Android, offline.**

No Android app opens a RootsMagic tree. Copy your .rmtree file to the phone and browse your whole family tree offline, wherever you are: at a cemetery, at the archives, at a family gathering.

## What it does

- Put a copy of your .rmtree file on the phone (USB cable, Google Drive, email) and open it; your file is never changed
- Every person, sorted by surname, with search by name or year
- Person page: names, facts and events with date and place, notes, parents, spouses, children and siblings
- Dates as you entered them: about, before, after, between, from…to, double dates
- Places, with every fact recorded at each place, and the small pictures stored in your tree
- Large trees open fine: tested with more than 11,000 people
- A fictional sample tree is included, to try it first

## Included without Pro

One tree, no limit on people, everything listed above.

## Pro: one purchase, yours for good

- Full photos from your pictures folder
- Ancestor chart, four generations
- Several trees on the phone
- Share a person's page as text

No subscription, no ads, no account.

## Privacy

Your tree stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no analytics, and Rootfold asks for no storage permission.

Rootfold is read-only: keep editing on your computer and copy the file again when you want the latest version. Reads family tree files made by RootsMagic (.rmtree).
Not affiliated with RootsMagic, Inc.
